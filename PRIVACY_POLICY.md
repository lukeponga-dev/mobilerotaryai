# Privacy Policy for RotorSense

_Last updated: December 2025_

## Introduction
RotorSense (“the App”) is a mobile-first diagnostic assistant designed for Mazda RX-8 owners. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

## Data Collection & Storage
- **Local Storage Only:**  
  All diagnostic sessions, including messages, images, and videos, are stored **only on your device’s local storage**.  
  RotorSense does not collect or store this data on its own servers.

- **API Processing:**  
  When you submit text, images, or videos for analysis, the content is sent to the **Google Gemini API** for processing.  
  Please refer to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how they handle your data.

## Data Deletion
- You can delete all diagnostic sessions at any time directly from your device.  
- Because RotorSense does not store your data on external servers, deleting local storage removes all records permanently.  
- For API-related data deletion, please contact Google directly.

## Sharing of Information
- RotorSense does not share, sell, or transmit your data to third parties.  
- Diagnostic reports exported to PDF remain under your control and are not uploaded by the App.

## Security
- Data is stored securely in your browser or device local storage.  
- No external databases or cloud services are used by RotorSense.

## Your Rights
- You control your data: delete sessions, clear local storage, or uninstall the App at any time.  
- For questions about API data, please consult Google’s privacy resources.

## How We Use Your Information

The data you provide is used for the following purposes:

-   To provide the core functionality of the diagnostic assistant.
-   To persist your diagnostic sessions for future reference on your device.
-   To improve the application's performance and features based on anonymous usage patterns.

## Data Storage and Security

Your session data is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This means your data remains on your device and is not uploaded to any server. You have full control over this data and can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's cache or by deleting sessions within the application.

We do not use cookies for tracking purposes.

## Third-Party Services

The application uses the Google Gemini API to process your queries and generate diagnostic responses. When you send a message, the content of that message (including text and any attached media) is sent to Google for processing. We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle your data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
